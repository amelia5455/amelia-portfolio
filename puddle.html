Seattle in November. The sky lies flat on the pavers and every drop breaks it into rings.

Dev Notes.

Written as a fragment shader: running-bond pavers, a puddle shaped from layered noise, and raindrops as decaying ring waves that bend the reflected sky. A click sends a pair of legs in jeans and red rainboots down from the top of the frame, drawn with signed distance fields and reflected in the water. Each landing pushes the water away, throws a crown and streaking spray, and sends out a heavy swell; the spray leaves its own small rings where it falls. Post processed with a shallow depth of field, a little bloom, film grain, and frequency modulated halftones.
